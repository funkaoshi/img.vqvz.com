My name is Ramanan Sivaranjan. Right now this site is simply a nicer way to look at my photos on Flickr. At some point this site will present a selection of my photos from Flickr. Or maybe the site will do both of these things.

You can browse through the photos on this site using the 'j' and 'k' keys on your keyboard. 'j' will advance to the next photo (and if needed the next page) and 'k' will move to the previous photo (and if needed the previous page). This is similar to what you might have seen in use on the Boston Globe's The Big Picture site, or in Gmail while browsing through your emails. You can read a much longer article on the creation of this website over on at funkaoshi.com.

The site was originally built using Sinatra, a (simple) Ruby web framework, with the heavy lifting done by the incredible Flickraw rubygem. It now runs on Flask, a similarly simple Python framework. The goal was to see if I can use Flickr as the backend for a photoblog. This is looking quite likely. I will probably use this code as a starting point for another project, Bloor and Lansdowne is Blansdowne.
